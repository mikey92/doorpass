How the door decides

  1. Presence · a press on the Ring doorbell at this door
  2. Permission · a booking for now (10 min early is fine)
  3. Identity · a tap on the member's pass within 60 s of the press

All three: the lock opens and re-locks after 10 s. Anything missing: you get a one-tap request; no answer in 90 s, it stays locked. Ring's Partner API is read-only, so the lock is opened through SwitchBot's API; Ring is the eyes and ears.
